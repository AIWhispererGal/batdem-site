EZMeeting
Beta

Meeting management for Los Angeles neighborhood councils. Agendas, minutes, postings, and records that stay inside the Brown Act by default — built for the volunteers who keep the city's most local layer of government running, and for the accessibility standards it's now required to meet.

QueueTime
Live on Zoom Marketplace

Speaker queue management for Zoom meetings. QueueTime gives chairs and clerks an ordered, visible queue for public comment and deliberation — raised hands become a fair, auditable speaking order instead of a scramble. The same structure works anywhere turn-taking matters: Model UN and debate clubs, classrooms, moderated panels, HOA boards.
